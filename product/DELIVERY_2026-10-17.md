# First Roadmap Delivery

Date: 2026-10-17. Scope: paper-only execution boundary and morning scanner recovery.

## Authority Change

David initially requested research/alerts only and automation off. Production's
disable endpoint was called and status confirmed OFF. David then clarified:
"we are just going to do paper trading, so its fine to automate the paper trades,
low risk, it doesnt matter, good for us to test". That newer instruction authorizes
paper automation, not live orders. No existing position was liquidated or broker
order canceled during this work.

## Delivered Scope

- Modes are paper or research; live is invalid. Alpaca is constructed with
  paper=True regardless of the legacy flag. Every mutation requires a verified
  paper connection and the actual paper transport URL. IBKR execution is disabled.
- Research mode blocks orders and automation at API, engine and adapter layers.
  Paper mode exposes Paper Buy/Short and Paper Auto labels. Authentication and
  account privacy remain unresolved; this is not full completion of KT-001.
- Pending paper entries reserve a daily slot immediately. Repeated sequential
  closes of an already-closed local Trade do not resubmit. Full concurrent
  idempotency, fill-based P&L and restart recovery remain unfinished.
- Russell filtering still means the existing IWB holdings proxy. Its unavailable
  Finnhub lookup was replaced with the issuer's dated CSV. No filter widening,
  extra stock-price floor, or looser setup/liquidity threshold was introduced.
- CSV validation rejects stale/future/missing dates, malformed tables and implausible
  equity counts/weights. Daily success caching and five-minute failure backoff
  avoid repeated requests without trusting unverified fallback data.
- Up to 100 filtered symbols receive minute-bar/RVOL initialization instead of 12.
  Independent lookups use bounded concurrency of four. Status exposes source/date,
  raw/filtered/active/qualified counts and unmonitored symbols.
- Failed scans clear actionable candidates; filter failures remove old watchlist
  rows. The UI distinguishes paused scanning, a closed market and no qualified setup.
- Provider diagnostics avoid Finnhub token-bearing exception URLs. Numeric settings
  are validated atomically and cannot alter execution mode.

## Evidence and Limits

The issuer download returned 1,016 common-ticker holdings dated 2026-10-15 and
passed integrity checks on October 17. Original Finnhub failure subtype was not
recoverable from the old generic response; entitlement denial versus provider
outage is not asserted as a proven cause. The same IWB proxy is preserved.

Local verification: 151 Python tests; seven Node tests; morning-mode and daily
Playwright suites passed. Tests cover paper transport enforcement, legacy live
flags, research API rejection, IBKR rejection, pending risk slots, atomic settings,
CSV failure cases, cache retry behavior, secret redaction, scan concurrency,
more-than-12 initialization and filter-failure cleanup.

No actual order was submitted as a test. Market-open alert timing and discretionary
setup quality need observed sessions. Implementation day is Saturday; an empty
qualified set while closed is not grounds to weaken thresholds.

## Production Verification

Commit 733eabb deployed successfully to Railway on October 17. Read-only status
and browser checks confirmed execution_mode=paper, paper_verified=true,
live_trading_allowed=false and auto_trade_enabled=true. No test order was sent.
The dashboard displayed PAPER ONLY, PAPER AUTO ON and P&L UNVERIFIED.

The issuer membership source was ready with 1,016 eligible common tickers dated
October 15. A completed scan took 1.158 seconds, with 26 source symbols, zero
filtered symbols and no reported source/filter errors. Market session was closed.
This verifies recovery of the failed membership dependency, not morning alert
quality or the latency of a populated scan. The universe starts with earnings
today plus market-wide top movers, then intersects enabled filters; it does not
rank every IWB holding. Investigate this discovery/coverage limitation in KT-016
before interpreting an empty intersection as absence of opportunities.
